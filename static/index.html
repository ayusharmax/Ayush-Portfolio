About

I am a Software Engineer focused on artificial intelligence and building software that solves real-world problems. I am interested in the engineering behind useful products — understanding problems, exploring possible solutions, and turning ideas into practical, reliable, and well-designed applications.

I have dedicated considerable time to researching and experimenting with AI from a practical perspective, exploring its applications alongside software development and product building. My exploration of AI vulnerabilities and the use of machine learning has shaped how I view AI as more than just a technology—as a tool that should be developed with people and their needs in mind. I tend to approach automation thoughtfully, prioritizing controlled outputs, clearly defined boundaries, and predictable behavior over hype-driven integrations.

My inclination toward backend engineering and system design stems from an interest in understanding how software works beneath the surface. I am particularly drawn to APIs, real-time systems, authentication, and data-driven applications where correctness and reliability matter. I enjoy understanding how systems fail, identifying edge cases, and designing solutions that remain clear, robust, and maintainable as they evolve. Rather than relying on black-box solutions, I prefer to understand what happens behind the scenes and the reasoning behind the technical decisions that shape a system.

As I continue developing my skills in AI and software engineering,I am focused on building increasingly capable projects and deepening my understanding of the technologies I work with. I am particularly interested in exploring ideas that combine intelligent systems with practical applications and have the potential to evolve into useful products.

In the long term, I aspire to build a technology startup of my own, combining engineering, product thinking, and entrepreneurship to turn meaningful ideas into real-world solutions. For now, my focus is on learning through building, strengthening my technical foundation, and developing the skills needed to take an idea from its earliest stages to a product that people can genuinely benefit from.
