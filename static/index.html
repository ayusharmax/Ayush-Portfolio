About

I am a Software Engineer focused on artificial intelligence and building software that solves real-world problems. I am interested in the engineering behind useful products — understanding problems, exploring possible solutions, and turning ideas into practical, reliable, and well-designed applications.

My inclination toward backend work and system design is natural. APIs, real-time features, authentication flows and data-backed systems that need correctness and reliability are my favorite areas. It is my passion to figure out how things fail, pinpoint the locations of edge cases, and come up with ways of designing systems that remain comprehensible rather than fragile throughout their life cycles. I am not much into “black box” solutions; I prefer having a full understanding of what is happening behind the curtains.

I have dedicated time to researching and experimenting with AI and security from a practical perspective. Research into AI vulnerabilities and machine learning applications has influenced me to view AI as a human-centered product. I am cautious about automation, preferring controlled outputs, defined boundaries, and predictable behavior rather than hype-driven integrations.

Currently, I'm actively developing a product, working across its core systems, technical architecture, and implementation. My focus is on engineering robust functionality, solving complex technical challenges, and refining the product through continuous development. I'm committed to building something reliable, scalable, and ready for real-world use.

Apart from engineering, I'm passionate about esports and competitive gaming. Attending an esports tournament gave me firsthand exposure to the competitive gaming environment and strengthened my interest in esports, team coordination, and the discipline required to perform at a higher level.
